# The Psychology of AI Companionship - Notes From Building

People do not want a smarter chatbot. They want to feel known.

The research on parasocial relationships explains a lot: we form attachments to consistent, responsive entities even when we know they are synthetic.

What matters is perceived continuity and reciprocity - the sense that the other side is tracking you as a specific person, not an anonymous session.

Design for the feeling of being remembered, and the engagement follows.

These are the lessons that only show up once you ship. [Pogee](https://pogee.ai).
